# 2701 service manual, cited

Source: ARP Pro Soloist Model 2701 / Pro-DGX 2720 Service Manual, doc 9001801, 3rd printing March 1977. Synthfool 49-page scan for ROM tables and flow charts. Archive.org 64-page scan for Board A/B/C schematics.

Do not treat Fricko or Cherry as this table.

## Signal path (SM 6.3, Board C block diagram p.25, schematic p.48 of the 64-page set)

Pulse from Board A goes through the pulse amp, then to resonator banks 1/2/3. Saw and/or pulse go through the mixer, then one or more of four series-RC highpasses, then the 4034, then the VCA.

Banks 1 and 2 feed the VCA only. Bank 3 feeds the VCF, the VCA, or both (ROM Z8).

Resonators see pulse only.

## Pulse (SM 6.1, ROM Z15 p.8)

Fixed widths from the six-stage divider: 1/14, 1/9, 1/64, 1/2, 2/11. Dynamic pulse is a separate converter, ADSR from Board B into Board A. ROM Z15 can assert more than one select at once.

Saw is six weighted divider taps, 64-step staircase (SM 6.1, DAFx 2019).

## Highpass (Board C schematic)

Each section is series C, 12k to ground. Enable bits are Z7 pins 1-4 (A, B, C, D).

| Section | C | fc = 1/(2 pi R C) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| D | 0.22 uF | 60 Hz |
| C | 0.047 uF | 282 Hz |
| B | 0.022 uF | 603 Hz |
| A | 0.005 uF | 2653 Hz |

## Resonators (Board C schematic)

Twin-T style, f0 = 1 / (2 pi R sqrt(C1 C2)). Same bank, two nets on: one interpolated peak, not two (Fricko; schematic sums into one op-amp per bank).

| Name | Bank | R | C1 | C2 | f0 Hz |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cello 2 | 1 | 100k | 120 pF | 0.01 uF | 1453 |
| Violin 2 | 1 | 100k | 150 pF | 0.01 uF | 1299 |
| English Horn | 2 | 120k | 180 pF | 0.01 uF | 988 |
| Cello 1 | 2 | 330k | 270 pF | 0.01 uF | 293 |
| Violin 3 | 2 | 68k | 50 pF | 0.01 uF | 3308 |
| Violin 1 | 3 | 150k | 330 pF | 0.01 uF | 585 |
| Cello 3 | 3 | 150k | 30 pF | 0.01 uF | 1937 |

Q is the bootstrapped op-amp around each bank, not a vocal-formant Q. DSP uses 2.2.

## Envelopes (SM 6.2, Board B schematic)

Discrete resistors, ROM Z1-Z3. Attack paths around 22k. Decay 100k / 220k / 560k. Release 100k / 220k / 680k. C3 is the ADSR cap; this printing does not print C3 on the parts extract we have, so times use 1 uF (tau_ms = R_kOhm). That is the one analog constant not read off a silkscreen.

## Brilliance / growl / wow (SM 6.2, 6.4)

Panel brilliance slider sits in the VCF CV path on Board A after Z6. Touch brilliance is a separate sum into Z6 on Board B. Growl is a 32 Hz astable on Board D, gated into a bandpass, into VCF CV. Wow forces maximum resonance.

## Octave (Z15 pins 1-2)

Down 1 and down 2 are independent. Both on = down 3. Tuba flow chart: "same as flute down 3 octaves."

## Flow charts checked against ROM bits

Oboe (code 10011): 1/14 pulse, Oboe resonator, bank 3 to VCF, ADSR to VCF, AR to VCA.
Tuba (code 10110): saw to HPF C, no resonators, ADSR to VCF, AR to VCA, down 3 octaves.
Flute: saw, no pulse select bits. ADSR to VCA (Board B Z1 pin 3, SM p.8, columns 8-11 = 0011).
Clarinet: 1/2 pulse.
Fuzz Guitar I: dynamic pulse.
